What does a Houston personal injury attorney do?

At its core, a Houston personal injury attorney exists to protect the legal rights of someone who has been injured, or the rights of a family that has lost a loved one, through no fault of their own.

Under the law, the person or company responsible for causing that injury or death is required to pay for the harm and losses that result from it. That includes medical expenses already incurred and medical care that will be needed in the future, lost income and the loss of future earning capacity, and the pain, suffering, grief, and emotional toll the injury or loss has caused. In a wrongful death case, that also includes the profound loss a family experiences when a loved one is taken from them. These are just some of the many categories of damages the law allows an injured person, or a surviving family, to recover.

How do you choose a Houston Personal Injury Attorney?

Choosing the right Houston personal injury or wrongful death attorney is one of the most important decisions you will make after a serious injury or the loss of a loved one, and it deserves real research before you sign anything.

Do not be afraid to ask direct questions. Ask about their trial results. Ask about the cases they have actually handled and how those cases were resolved. Ask how many seven and eight figure settlements or verdicts they have secured, both in the last year and over the last five years. A track record of real numbers tells you far more than a website ever will. Just as important is asking how the firm actually works day to day. Will you be speaking with your attorney directly, or will most of your communication go through a case manager or a paralegal you have never met? Do you have your attorney’s direct phone number?

How can a Houston Personal Injury Attorney help me?

A personal injury attorney in Houston can help you navigate the legal process after a serious injury and determine whether you have a viable claim.

These cases often involve identifying who may be legally responsible for the harm you’ve suffered and understanding what types of damages and compensation may be available under the law. Attorneys assist with gathering evidence, preserving records, communicating with insurance companies, and advising you on how to move forward after an accident. If your case qualifies, your attorney may also represent you in pre-litigation negotiations or formal court proceedings. Working with a personal injury attorney can help protect your rights and ensure that the legal process accounts for the full extent of your injuries.

What does it cost to hire a Houston Personal Injury Attorney?

Many personal injury attorneys, including those at our firm, work on a contingency fee basis.

This means you do not pay any upfront attorney fees. Instead, the attorney receives a percentage of the final recovery if the case is resolved successfully through settlement or judgment. The specific terms of this arrangement are discussed during the initial consultation. Clients are typically not responsible for attorney fees unless compensation is recovered. To learn more about what to expect when hiring a personal injury attorney , visit our detailed guide.

What types of cases do personal injury attorneys handle?

Personal injury attorneys handle a wide range of legal matters involving injuries caused by the negligent or wrongful actions of others.

These cases often include incidents such as car accidents, truck crashes , workplace injuries, product liability claims, premises liability, dog-related injuries, and slip-and-fall accidents. In each case, the attorney’s role is to determine whether a legal duty was breached and whether the injured person may be entitled to pursue compensation under the law.
